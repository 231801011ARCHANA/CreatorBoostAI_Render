Essential Cooking Techniques

Important techniques include boiling, simmering, steaming, sauteing, pan-frying, roasting, baking, grilling, braising, stir-frying, blanching, and poaching.

Boiling uses high-temperature water; simmering uses gentler heat. Sauteing generally cooks ingredients relatively quickly with a small amount of fat. Roasting and baking use dry heat, while steaming uses hot vapor.

Stir-frying commonly uses high heat and quick movement, so ingredients are often prepared in advance. Blanching briefly cooks food and is followed by cooling for suitable preparations.
