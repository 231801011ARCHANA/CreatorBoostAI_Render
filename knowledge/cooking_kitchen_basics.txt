Kitchen Basics and Food Safety

Useful beginner tools include a cutting board, chef's knife, small knife, measuring cups and spoons, mixing bowls, saucepan, frying pan, baking tray, spatula, tongs, colander, storage containers, and a kitchen thermometer when appropriate.

Knife safety includes a stable cutting surface, safe finger positioning, attention while cutting, and appropriate knife storage.

Cross-contamination is an important food-safety concept. Raw foods and ready-to-eat foods should be handled in ways that reduce transfer of harmful microorganisms.

Cooking temperature matters for food safety and food quality. Storage also matters: perishable foods should be stored under suitable conditions and containers should be clean and appropriate.
