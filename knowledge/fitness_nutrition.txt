Fitness Nutrition Basics

Nutrition is an important part of an overall fitness routine. Beginner nutrition content should explain basic concepts without promoting extreme diets or claiming that one eating pattern works for everyone.

Protein contributes to maintenance and repair of body tissues. Sources include eggs, dairy products, legumes, beans, lentils, fish, meat, tofu, and other protein-containing foods.

Carbohydrates provide an important source of energy. Sources include rice, oats, potatoes, whole grains, and fruits.

Dietary fats have important roles in normal body functions. Sources include nuts, seeds, oils, avocados, and other fat-containing foods.

Fiber is found in vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains. A varied diet can provide different nutrients and food components.

Hydration is relevant before, during, and after physical activity. Needs vary with activity, environment, and individual circumstances.

Meal-planning content can demonstrate combining a protein source, carbohydrate source, vegetables or fruit, and an appropriate source of fats.
