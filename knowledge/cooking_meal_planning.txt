Meal Planning and Preparation

Meal planning helps organize shopping, cooking, ingredient use, preparation time, and leftovers.

Useful topics include weekly meal plans, shopping lists, batch cooking, meal preparation, leftovers, variety, quick meals, budget-conscious planning, freezer-friendly meals, portion planning, and ingredient organization.

A simple plan can list meals for the week and identify ingredients shared across dishes. Batch preparation may include washing and cutting suitable ingredients, preparing staples, cooking components ahead, and storing them appropriately.

Using overlapping ingredients can reduce food waste. People with limited time may benefit from fewer-ingredient recipes, shorter preparation, and make-ahead components.
