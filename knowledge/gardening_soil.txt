Gardening Soil and Containers

Growing media provide physical support for roots and influence water availability, drainage, and nutrient access. Choosing suitable soil or growing media is important for plant growth.

Different plants have different preferences for moisture retention and drainage. A growing medium that works for one plant may not be ideal for another.

Container gardening requires suitable drainage. Containers should have drainage holes, and the growing medium should allow appropriate movement of water and air around roots.

Compost and other organic materials can be used depending on the growing method and plant requirements. Adding more material is not automatically better.

Soil advice should consider the plant and growing environment. There is no single growing medium that is optimal for every plant.
