Common Cooking Mistakes

Common beginner mistakes include not reading the full recipe, skipping preparation, overcrowding a pan, using unsuitable heat, inconsistent cutting, adding too much seasoning, overcooking, undercooking, ignoring food safety, and storing food incorrectly.

Overcrowding can reduce browning because ingredients release moisture and reduce available cooking surface. High heat does not always mean faster or better cooking.

Consistent cutting helps food cook more evenly. Ingredient substitutions should consider the ingredient's role, such as flavor, moisture, structure, or acidity.
